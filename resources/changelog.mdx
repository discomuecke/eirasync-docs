# Changelog

All notable changes to EiraSync.

## August 2026

### Added

- Paddle.com Market Ltd. added as Merchant of Record for billing and subscription management.
- New billing page with subscription status and lifecycle banners.
- Self-service account deletion.
- Email preference settings.
- German dashboard translation (full i18n support alongside English).
- Redesigned setup URI onboarding flow, linked directly to the illustrated Quick Start guide.

### Changed

- Improved onboarding experience.
- Updated account management and subscription handling.

---

## August 2026 — Initial Release

### Added

- Initial public release of EiraSync.
- Managed CouchDB hosting for Obsidian LiveSync, hosted exclusively in the EU (Germany, via Hetzner).
- End-to-end, zero-knowledge encrypted synchronization — encryption keys never leave your devices.
- Vault management dashboard, including creation, deletion, and storage usage monitoring.
- Automated, encrypted nightly off-site backups.
- 14-day free trial (no credit card required, 1 vault, 3 GB storage).
- Paid plans with support for up to three vaults.
- Automatic read-only mode for 30 days after subscription cancellation or expiry.
- Automatic, permanent data deletion after the read-only retention period.

---

## July 2026 — Alpha

### Added

- Initial dual-plane architecture: Supabase control plane, Hetzner CouchDB data plane.
- Managed CouchDB infrastructure setup and validation.
- First successful connection between the Obsidian LiveSync plugin and the managed backend.
- End-to-end encryption data flow verified to confirm the zero-knowledge design in practice.
- Passwordless authentication via Magic Link, implemented through Cloudflare Workers.
