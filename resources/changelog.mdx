# Changelog

All updates, improvements, and bug fixes for EiraSync.

## August 2026 — Account & Billing Updates

- **Billing:** Migrated payment provider to Paddle (Merchant of Record). Added a new billing page, subscription status, and lifecycle banners to the dashboard.
- **Account:** Added self-service account deletion and email preference toggles *(rolling out)*.
- **Internationalization:** The dashboard is now fully available in both English and German.
- **Onboarding:** Redesigned the setup-URI flow in the dashboard and linked it to the illustrated Quick Start guide.

## August 2026 — MVP Launch

EiraSync is officially live. The initial release includes our complete zero-knowledge setup for Obsidian LiveSync.

- **Infrastructure & Security:** E2EE CouchDB hosting on the Hetzner data plane (Germany). Includes automated, encrypted nightly offsite backups.
- **Vault Management:** Self-service dashboard for creating, managing, and deleting vaults, including storage usage monitoring.
- **Subscriptions:** 14-day trial (no credit card, 1 vault, 3 GB). Seamless conversion to paid plans (up to 3 vaults).
- **Data Lifecycle:** Automated 30-day "read-only" mode after cancellation or expiry, followed by a strict data purge.
