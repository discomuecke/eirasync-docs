## August 2026 — Account, Billing & Onboarding

### Added

- **Billing Infrastructure:** Paddle.com Market Ltd. added as Merchant of Record for subscription management.
- **Billing UI:** New dashboard page featuring subscription status and lifecycle banners.
- **Account Management:** Introduced self-service account deletion and email preference settings.
- **Localization:** Dashboard is now fully translated into German (full i18n support alongside English).
- **Onboarding:** Redesigned the setup URI flow, linking directly to the illustrated Quick Start guide.

---

## August 2026 — MVP Launch

### Added

- **Public Release:** Initial public launch of EiraSync.
- **Infrastructure:** Managed CouchDB hosting for Obsidian LiveSync, hosted exclusively in the EU (Germany, via Hetzner).
- **Security:** End-to-end, zero-knowledge encrypted synchronization (encryption keys never leave your devices).
- **Vault Management:** Dashboard for vault creation, deletion, and storage usage monitoring.
- **Data Protection:** Automated, encrypted nightly off-site backups (EU-hosted, Hetzner Storage Box).
- **Plans:** 14-day free trial (no credit card required, 1 vault, 3 GB storage) and paid plans supporting up to three vaults.
- **Data Lifecycle:** Automatic read-only mode for 30 days after subscription cancellation or expiry, followed by permanent data deletion.

---

## July 2026 — Alpha

### Added

- **Architecture:** Initial dual-plane setup deployed (Supabase control plane, Hetzner CouchDB data plane).
- **Core Sync:** Managed CouchDB infrastructure validated with the first successful connection to the Obsidian LiveSync plugin.
- **Zero-Knowledge:** End-to-end encryption data flow verified in practice.
- **Authentication:** Passwordless authentication via Magic Link implemented through Cloudflare Workers.
