Connect Obsidian to EiraSync — in a few steps.

<Info>
  Copy your setup link from the dashboard, install the LiveSync plugin, paste the link — done. Follow each section in order.
</Info>

## 1. Get your setup link

### In the EiraSync dashboard

Sign in, open your vault, and click **"Generate new setup link"**.

Copy the setup link and URI passphrase — you will need both in Obsidian.

<Tip>
  Save them now. **"Regenerate"** disconnects all devices.
</Tip>

## 2. Install the LiveSync plugin

Install [Self-hosted LiveSync](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-livesync) — the official Obsidian community plugin (by vrtmrz).

### Install & enable in Obsidian

1. **Settings → Community plugins → Browse** → search "LiveSync".
2. Install **"Self-hosted LiveSync"** and click **"Enable"** — [plugin page](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-livesync). Same steps on your phone.

## 3. Set up your first device

### Follow the wizard

1. Choose **"I am setting this up for the first time"**.
2. Keep **"Use a Setup URI"** as the connection method and continue.

<Frame>
  ![Setup URI as connection method](https://eirasync.app/setup-tutorial/desktop-device-a-setup-uri.png)
</Frame>

### Paste link and passphrase

1. Paste setup link and URI passphrase from EiraSync → **"Test Settings and Continue"**.
2. When asked about uploading: **"Yes"** — your notes go to the cloud.

<Frame>
  ![Enter setup URI and passphrase](https://eirasync.app/setup-tutorial/desktop-device-a-enter-uri.png)
</Frame>

## 4. Add another device

Same setup link as device 1 — do not generate a new one in the dashboard.

### Second computer

1. Install LiveSync, choose **"adding a device to an existing setup"** in the wizard.
2. Paste the same setup link and passphrase. When asked about uploading: **"No"**.

### Phone via QR code

1. [Self-hosted LiveSync](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/obsidian-livesync) install, choose **"adding a device to an existing setup"**, then **"Scan a QR Code"**.
2. On your first device: **Settings → Self-hosted LiveSync → "Setup" tab → "Show QR code" → "For your eyes only"**.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone, enter the passphrase.

## Nice work — you did it!

Your notes now sync across your devices. Check the [dashboard](https://eirasync.app/dashboard) anytime to see your vault status.

## Good to know

### Zero-knowledge architecture

Your notes are encrypted on your device before they reach the server. EiraSync only stores encrypted data — we cannot read your notes, even if we wanted to. Only you hold the key.

### One link for all devices

Desktop and phone share the same setup link.

### "Not a server admin"

You can ignore this — normal for your private vault.
